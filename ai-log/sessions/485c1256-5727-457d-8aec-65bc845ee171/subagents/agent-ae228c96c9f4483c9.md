1. `ActivityHealthReport.cs:6-16` and `ActivityHealthResponse.cs` — The `Report` and `Response` records are a near-identical pair, split only to rename and flatten. `WeekStart`/`WeekEnd` become `Week{Start,End}`, `DataAsOfUtc` becomes `DataAsOf`, `TimeZoneId` becomes `Timezone`, and `HealthStatus` becomes a string. The same duplication runs through `AccountTotal`/`SummaryResponse`, `LocationHealth`/`LocationHealthResponse`, `BaselineAssessment`/`BaselineResponse` and `AccountListItem`/`AccountResponse`. That is 5 record pairs plus `WeekRangeResponse`, and they need 3 `ToResponse` extension classes plus a `ToQuery` class. The cost is about 12 files and a mapper with no behaviour of its own.
   - Simpler form: keep the Response records as the only wire shape. This is NOT pinned as long as the JSON stays identical, but it is a layering decision, because Core would then own wire names.
   - Cheaper option that stays pinned-safe: have `BaselineAssessment` carry the flat `weeksUsed/median/low/high` and expose `Baseline` as a nested record once, and drop `BaselineResponse`. The JSON shape is unchanged.

2. `AccountTotal.cs:5` versus `LocationHealth.cs:3` — These are the same data. `AccountTotal(Count, Baseline)` is `LocationHealth` without `Location`. `SummaryResponse` and `LocationHealthResponse` duplicate each other in the same way.

   - Not pinned.
